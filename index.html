FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about emergency and after hours dental care in the Bronx.

Are you really open as an after hours dentist in the Bronx?

Yes. We provide after hours and 24 hour emergency dental care. Call 347-428-5121 any time, day or night, and our team will arrange the fastest possible appointment.

Do I need an appointment for an emergency dental visit?

Calling ahead is recommended so we can prepare for your arrival and give first aid advice, but we always make room for urgent cases and same day patients.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe toothache, swelling or abscess, a knocked-out, broken or chipped tooth, uncontrolled bleeding, and facial trauma involving the teeth or mouth are all dental emergencies.

Is emergency dental care affordable?

We focus on affordable emergency dentistry and explain costs before treatment begins. Call us to ask about payment options and insurance.

What should I do while I wait to be seen?

Rinse gently with warm water, use a cold compress on the cheek, keep any broken tooth pieces, and avoid placing aspirin on the gum. Call us right away for guidance.
